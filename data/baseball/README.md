# Baseball knowledge data

`knowledge.json` is generated by `scripts/import-lahman.ts` from the Lahman
Baseball Database CSV files. Do not edit it by hand; re-run the importer.

**Current file:** built from a Baseball Databank snapshot (the Chadwick Baseball
Bureau's distribution of the Lahman data), seasons through 2021, obtained from
github.com/cbwinslow/baseballdatabank (a fork of chadwickbureau/baseballdatabank).
It holds:
- full season detail for the 10 card players;
- a compact career record (totals, awards, All-Star years, Hall of Fame) for
  all 20,673 people in the database.
